Cosplay with 3D Printing

Practical notes for engineers, designers and people making their next idea real.

Fit the person, not just the model

Measure the wearer and test a small section of the prop or armour. Account for padding, movement and fastening points.

Split large pieces deliberately

Place joints where they can be reinforced or hidden. Alignment features help sections fit together accurately.

Plan weight and finish

Large solid parts can become heavy. Review walls and infill, then allow time for assembly, sanding and paint.
