Our Journey

2015

Company Founded

Started as a small desktop 3D printing service in a garage.

2017

First Industrial Lab

Acquired our first fleet of industrial SLS and SLA printers.

2019

Expanded Materials

Introduced carbon-fiber composites and metal composite printing.

2022

Advanced Automation

Launched our automated quoting and printability analysis software.

2024

Creative Studio Launch

Opened a dedicated division for artists, creators, and hobbyists.

2026

Global Innovation Platform

Connecting designers with local print farms globally.
