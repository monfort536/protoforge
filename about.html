ABOUT PROTOFORGE

Good ideas deserve
to become real things.

ProtoForge brings digital design, additive manufacturing and practical engineering together, from a first concept to a finished custom part.

YOUR ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING PARTNER

A shorter distance
between idea and object.

We help engineers, designers and creators understand how a part will be made, which material fits the job and what to review before production.

Our mission is to make professional prototyping approachable. Our vision is a development process where people can test physical ideas early and manufacture only what they need.

CAPABILITIES, CONNECTED

The equipment is only part of the story.

FDM

FDM 3D Printing

Build durable and cost-effective parts layer by layer using thermoplastic filament. A versatile choice for functional geometry.

Enclosures, fixtures, concept models
PLA · PETG · ABS · TPU
Explore service ↗
SLA

SLA / Resin Printing

Cure liquid resin into detailed parts with smooth surfaces. Make small features and presentation models easier to evaluate.

Miniatures, visual prototypes, detailed models
Photopolymer resin
Explore service ↗
SLS

SLS Printing

Fuse nylon powder into complex shapes. Surrounding powder supports the part, opening up interlocking and internal geometry.

Nylon housings, complex functional components
