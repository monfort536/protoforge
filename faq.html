Support & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our materials, printing process, shipping, and pricing.

We currently accept .STL, .OBJ, and .STEP files. For the best results and quickest turnaround time, we highly recommend exporting your models in .STL format with a high resolution mesh.

Standard production time is typically 3-5 business days depending on the size and complexity of the print. We also offer expedited 24-hour and 48-hour priority printing for an additional fee.

For outdoor applications where UV resistance and temperature stability are required, we strongly recommend ASA or PETG. Standard PLA will warp in direct sunlight and degrade over time outdoors.

Yes! We offer tiered pricing for batch orders. Discounts typically begin at quantities of 10+ for the same part, scaling up to mass-manufacturing pricing for 1,000+ units. Please contact our sales team for a custom quote.

Orders can be cancelled or modified within 2 hours of placement. Once an order enters the 'In Production' stage and the machines are running, we cannot cancel or refund the order due to the custom nature of the service.
