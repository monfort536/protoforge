Detailed Material Profiles

PLA

Polylactic Acid. The most common material, easy to print, great for visual models.

Finish: Smooth/Glossy
Starting from: $0.15 / gram

PETG

Polyethylene Terephthalate Glycol. Strong, slightly flexible, water-resistant.

Finish: Glossy
Starting from: $0.20 / gram

ABS

Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene. High impact resistance, can be vapor smoothed.

Finish: Matte (Can be glossed)
Starting from: $0.20 / gram

TPU

Thermoplastic Polyurethane. Highly flexible, rubber-like material.

Finish: Textured
Starting from: $0.35 / gram

Resin

Photopolymer Resin. Extreme detail and smooth surfaces, ideal for jewelry/miniatures.

Finish: Ultra Smooth
Starting from: $0.40 / ml

Nylon

Polyamide. Exceptional durability, low friction, chemical resistant.

Finish: Matte/Rough
Starting from: $0.50 / gram

Carbon Fiber

CF infused polymers. Extreme stiffness and lightweight.

Finish: Matte Dark
Starting from: $0.80 / gram

Metal Composite

Polymers infused with copper, brass, or steel for weight and aesthetics.

Finish: Metallic
Starting from: $0.70 / gram

Wood Filled

PLA mixed with wood fibers. Smells like wood, can be sanded and stained.

Finish: Wood Texture
Starting from: $0.30 / gram
